No ticket. The behavior the message describes is real. `SubprocessExec.run` (squatch/seams.py:123-125) leaves stdout/stderr unpiped when `timeout=None`, so the child inherits them, but stdin is `asyncio.subprocess.DEVNULL` whenever no `stdin_path` is passed. `Drain._exec` (squatch/drain.py:459-462) passes no `stdin_path`, so the self-upgrade handoff child reads stdin from /dev/null. The seam docstring (seams.py:114-115), the drain module docstring (drain.py:51) and section 18 (SQUATCH_PLAN.md:653) all call this 'inherited stdio'. That wording is loose for stdin, but it causes no harm today. The drain is headless and never reads stdin, so the only stdio that matters to an operator is the output stream, and that is inherited. The message makes its own fix conditional on a daemon-era verb needing a tty. No such verb exists, and the message gives no evidence (evidence: None) and comes from `bootstrap-ingest`. Under D10 a docstring note or a new inherited-stdin mode would be speculative. A precise fix would also have to change the plan wording at :653 first and then the two docstrings, which is a plan-plus-regeneration change with no incident behind it. A tombstone does not fit. seams-kill-transport-timeout and seams-kill-poll-flake cover the kill path, cli-prompt-path-reuse covers `stdin_path` for CLI prompts, and decisions 000203/000204 cover what the handoff carries. None of them covers stdin in the unbounded mode. Reopen if any verb or handoff child needs to read interactive stdin or a tty, if an operator is misled by the 'inherited stdio' wording, or if the plan is edited at section 18's handoff sentence for another reason, since the wording could then be made exact in the same change.

Evidence: squatch/seams.py:114-115 docstring 'inherited-stdio mode'; seams.py:123-125 pipe=None only for stdout/stderr, stdin=DEVNULL when stdin_path is None; squatch/drain.py:459-462 `_exec` calls the seam with timeout=None and no stdin_path; drain.py:51 and SQUATCH_PLAN.md:653 say 'inherited stdio'; the message's evidence field is None and its origin is bootstrap-ingest.
