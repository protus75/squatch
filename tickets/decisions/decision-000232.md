No ticket. The merged code does what the plan says. The message reads the plan as letting only the diagnosis budget block the call, but plan section 11.3 item 1 (SQUATCH_PLAN.md:1176), 'Caps fire first, before any model call', says: 'If any cap is spent, terminate to the human Reject queue without invoking the model', and the recorded reason must name the spent cap verbatim. `Diagnoser.diagnose` (squatch/diagnose.py:135) calls `spent(...)`. That function walks every member of `SPINE_CAPS = (retry, diagnosis, premise_bounce, infra)` (squatch/caps.py:27, :63-70), and on a hit it records a `skipped` call with that cap-naming reason. So a stem whose retry cap is spent skips diagnosis on purpose. This is not a divergence from the plan. Narrowing the skip to the diagnosis cap would change the plan rule and regenerate merged spine-diagnosis without any failure behind it, and the message gives no evidence (origin bootstrap-ingest), so under D10 that would be speculative. The operator also does not lose the record they need at the Reject queue. The final attempt's harvest still lands in `tickets/<stem>/attempts/<n>/` before the diagnosis step, and earlier attempts that had budget left were diagnosed and carry `diagnosis.json` lessons. The open drain-parked-detail ticket already points the parked report at the attempt directory when no diagnosis.json is on disk. A tombstone does not fit: spine-diagnosis's rendered Goal does not cover the scope of the cap pre-check, and no rendered decision does either. Reopen if an operator's Reject-queue triage of a retry-spent stem stalls because the final attempt has no lessons, or if plan section 11.3 item 1 is edited for another reason.
