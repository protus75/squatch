No ticket. The message says no seed can cite section 19 within the render bound, so seeds have to cite the owning sections instead. The committed tickets show otherwise. Fourteen tickets carry a `- section 19` Plan contract bullet, and every one of them rendered, dispatched and merged: requisition-review-call/-author/-seed, invariant-auditor, phase2-exit, shakeout-report and all eight shakeout groups. So citing section 19 is feasible in practice and is not banned outright. Section 19 has grown since the message was filed and is now about 101k characters (SQUATCH_PLAN.md lines 1447-1510). A future seed that combines it with heavy Context could therefore go over a bound. That case is already caught where tickets are authored. The merged requisition-review path measures each authored or seeded ticket's Implement render at the tightest `max` bound with a 0.75 headroom (`_measure`, squatch/requisition.py:280-291, REQ_RENDER_HEADROOM at :30), and it snags or RMAs an over-bound ticket before commit (see decision-000216). The paved road for a too-large ticket is already the snag re-prompt: cite the owning sections instead. Neither proposed fix is needed. A plan rule that section 19 is never cited would contradict fourteen merged, working tickets. Per-section injection bounds would add a second bounding mechanism next to the one whole-render bound in specs.py:145-150. With no incident behind either (evidence: None, origin bootstrap-ingest), both are speculative under D10. A tombstone does not fit: requisition-review-call owns the measurement, but no rendered Goal or decision covers section 19 citation sizing by name. Reopen if requisition review snags or RMAs a ticket over_bound and the only cause is a section 19 citation, or if an Implement render that cites section 19 is refused over_bound at dispatch.
