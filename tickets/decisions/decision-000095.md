No ticket. The behaviour the message asks Phase 1 to decide was settled when the code merged, so the question is not open in the code. What is missing is one line of plan text, and engine tickets cannot write plan text. Here is how it works now. Only writing surfaces get a workspace: `Driver` passes `worktree=workspace if stage.surface in WRITING_SURFACES else None` (squatch/driver.py:126). `CliClient.call_resolved` uses that worktree when one is given and otherwise falls back to its construction-time `cwd` (squatch/providers.py:398, :425). Review never needs the candidate branch's tree. specs/review.md renders the candidate branch's unified diff against its merge base as the `diff` data block (:63-74) and tells the reviewer to judge the diff on its own (:51-58). That is the message's second option, 'the rendered diff in its prompt', and it is already built. Running review inside the worktree as well would give review a second source of truth, which the no-dual-path and anti-bloat rules forbid. The gap left is in the plan text. Section 6 says nothing about the cwd for a read-only surface, so a plan regeneration could pick something different, such as a worktree cwd for review. This is the same kind of plan/code wording gap that decisions -000072, -000074, -000079, -000080 and -000091 sent to the operator. No rendered ticket or decision covers read-only surface cwd. The open config-*, gates-*, journal-*, seams-*, lockfile- and effects- tickets don't touch it, and tombstone-000094 is about kill and pgid binding. A tombstone would therefore be wrong. Operator action: in section 6, state that a non-writing surface runs at the configured checkout root with `worktree=None` and gets its subject (the diff, the ticket, the harvest) only as rendered prompt data. Reopen in any of these cases: a plan regeneration gives a read-only surface a worktree cwd; a review or diagnose finding depends on files that are not in its rendered data; or a read-only surface's sandbox (codex `--sandbox read-only`, SQUATCH_PLAN.md:328) turns out to read main's tree in a way that contradicts the rendered diff.

Evidence: squatch/driver.py:126 (worktree only for WRITING_SURFACES); squatch/providers.py:398,425 (construction cwd fallback); specs/review.md:51-58,63-74 (diff rendered as data, reviewed on its own); SQUATCH_PLAN.md:328 (read-only sandbox for non-implement codex surfaces).
