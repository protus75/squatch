No ticket. Half of the message is out of date. The `Filesystem` protocol in squatch/seams.py:38-65 has already grown `read` (:55) and `list` (:59), alongside `write`, `replace`, `unlink`, `publish` and `remove`, so it does not 'only expose write/replace'. The other half is correct: status.py, runner.py and tickets.py still read ticket and plan bytes through raw `Path.read_text()`/`is_file()`/`exists()`. But those three files are not special. The same raw read pattern appears about 113 times across 22 engine modules, including stages.py (26), tickets.py (14), runner.py (11), merge.py (9) and box.py (8). Moving only the three named sites would leave the engine with two read paths, which the no-dual-path rule forbids. Moving all of them would be a wide refactor, and nothing has earned it: no test needs to fake a read, the message cites no evidence, and the engine runs these reads against real disk in tmp-path fixtures today. That makes it speculative under the build-the-simplest-thing rule. The real open question is policy. Section 15 and the section 17 conduct row (SQUATCH_PLAN.md:1393) say filesystem access goes through the seam and is 'never called raw', and they make no read exemption. Whether reads are exempt, or must migrate when a test first needs a faked read, is plan wording that only the operator can settle. Decisions -000105, -000113, -000115, -000116, -000119, -000120, -000122, -000131 and -000132 went to the operator for the same reason. No rendered work or decision covers the scope of the filesystem seam. The control-inbox seam clarification (plan :1531) added `publish`, not a read policy, and status-projection only made status read-only. A tombstone would therefore be wrong. Optional operator action: in section 15, either state that whole-file reads and existence probes may stay raw `Path` calls until a test needs to fake one, and then migrate that module's reads to `Filesystem.read` in one change, or require every engine read to use the seam and author one migration deliverable. Then regenerate. Reopen if a test needs to fake a read or an existence check, if a regeneration moves part of the engine's reads onto `Filesystem.read` and leaves the rest raw, or if the operator settles the section 15 wording.

Evidence: squatch/seams.py:38-65 (Filesystem protocol already declares read and list); raw read_text/is_file/exists hits: about 113 across 22 squatch/ modules (stages.py 26, tickets.py 14, runner.py 11, merge.py 9, box.py 8, status.py 5); SQUATCH_PLAN.md:176 and :1393 (seam rule, no read exemption stated); message evidence: None.
