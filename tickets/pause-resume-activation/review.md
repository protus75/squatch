## Summary
The diff wires a shared control runtime, but the admission hold spins without yielding and its red streak misfires. Merge holds are not journaled, and rehydration misreads merge releases as dispatch releases. Two acceptance criteria are unmet because tests/test_mergequeue.py and tests/test_daemon_composition.py were never changed.

The file is not in the diff (check_report changed_files omits it), so the literal no-pause/no-hold assertions the ticket asked to migrate were not migrated, and no test exercises AdmissionHold. That breaks the no-preemption requirement ('neither cancels in-flight work'). (paved road: Yield between polls through the injected clock/sleep seam, or have the waiter block on a real wake signal.
